Rutgers Accounting Society (RAS) – Newark Chapter is a premier student-led organization at Rutgers Business School dedicated to preparing students for careers in accounting and related fields. Our mission is to help students rise above the crowd by creating opportunities, building networks, and equipping members with the technical and soft skills needed to become emerging leaders. Through professional development workshops, networking events, mentorship opportunities, and community engagement, RAS connects students with top firms, alumni, and faculty while fostering a supportive community for academic and career success

01 — About RAS

A student org runs like the professionals

The purpose of our organization is to help Rutgers–Newark students rise above the crowd by exploring the full scope of the accounting profession. Our mission is to connect members to professionals across various sectors within the areas of public accounting, government, financial regulation, and industry, while building hands-on experience through community service and professional development.

01

Diverse career exposure

Access to professionals well beyond Big 4 public accounting, including the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), financial regulation (NFA), and real estate (EisnerAmper).

02

Professional development

Resume workshops, interview prep, and CPA exam guidance by upperclassmen and firm professionals who've been through the same recruiting cycle.

03

Hands-on community service

Our VITA partnership with the Ironbound Community Corporation gives members real experience preparing free tax returns for low-income and elderly Newark residents.
